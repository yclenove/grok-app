1. **P1 — The ACL filter admits authority to grant the forbidden permissions.** `scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:1635`, `:1640`, `:1658`, `:1919`, `:1924`; effects at `:1682`, `:2354`.

   An inherited ACL granting another user `search,readsecurity,writesecurity` passes every new check: `writesecurity` is absent from the rejected permission list. That user can change the ACL to grant themselves add/delete rights after validation. They can then replace M between its identity check and pathname `rmdir`, causing deletion of a directory this attempt did not create; alternatively they can plant `volume-receipt.tmp` as a symlink before the receipt's truncating open. This is another principal, outside the accepted same-user concurrency residual.

   The actual filter returned **allow** for both ordinary and inherited `user:aclwriter ... allow writesecurity`, **block** for `allow add_file,delete`, and **allow** for the measured `0: group:everyone deny delete`. Thus the measured deny format is fixed, but exclusion of other namespace writers is incomplete.

   **Minimal fix:** reject non-owner/non-root ACL authority to change permissions (`writesecurity`), including inherited entries. Prefer an explicit allowlist of understood read-only permissions for those principals so unknown mutating permissions fail closed. Keep accepting the measured deny entry. Add an ACL fixture with search plus inherited `writesecurity` and assert refusal before image creation.

2. **P2 — C9 can accept success before its bounded capture readers finish.** `scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:915`, `:1039`, `:1043`, `:2198`.

   The two process-substitution `head` workers have no retained PIDs or completion checks. Waiting for SSH does not wait for them. A producer can write more than CAP, exit zero after its bytes enter the pipe, and leave the reader descheduled between its last read and final write. The post-SSH size check then sees less than CAP, sets `G_OV=0`, and C9 accepts the valid terminal record. When the reader resumes, the file reaches CAP after the acceptance check. Capture-worker failure is likewise not checked.

   A focused probe used the actual `ssh_bg`, `wait_guest`, `m_run_c9`, and judge definitions, synthetic valid result/space evidence, and a bounded reader that delayed only its final write. The producer successfully wrote **70,000 bytes** and exited zero. C9 returned **`ok`, `G_ST=0`, `G_OV=0`, `G_TO=0`** while the capture held **57,344 bytes**; it subsequently reached **65,536**. Only IO boundaries/capture paths were substituted. This demonstrates the missing completion dependency, not Mini scheduling measurements.

   **Minimal fix:** retain and supervise both capture-reader PIDs, then require confirmed successful drain/exit before the final overflow check and any successful verdict. Bound drain/termination/reap by the existing deadline; incomplete or failed capture means unknown. Add the delayed-final-write scenario with a zero-exit producer and valid record. An extra sleep or another unsynchronized size check does not establish completion.
