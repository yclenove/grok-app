1. **P1 / must-fix — An unreadable ledger readback becomes affirmative proof that a prerequisite write landed.** `src/disposable/tart/machine/io.rs:416`, `:430`, `:443`; `src/disposable/tart/machine/steps.rs:270`; `src/disposable/tart/machine/recover.rs:88`.

   **Failure scenario:** An in-memory `AttemptLedger` fake returns unwritten `ReplyLost` for `Append(StartIssued)`, then `TimedOut` for its readback. `read_back` converts that error to `Ok(true)`. Consequently `start` returns success and issues the runner start even though the row contains only `CloneIssued`. Drop the attempt, then recover with a complete list omitting the name. Recovery sees no `StartIssued`, treats the VM as never started, seals the row, and returns `cleanup_confirmed=true`, `recovery_required=false`, and followups allowed. Neither Tart nor Softnet exit was proved. This reproduces the false cleanup confirmation that the new started-VM distinction was meant to prevent.

   This is a legal, finite fake failure sequence, requiring no transport, concurrency, or deadline violation. The same optimistic branch also permits clone after an identity write whose durability could not be established. The standard `FakeLedger` cannot script read errors other than expiry/not-found, which is why the registered checks miss it.

   **Minimal fix:** Propagate an unknown readback as an error; only successful readback satisfying `present` may confirm a lost write. Refuse clone/start when their prerequisite cannot be confirmed. Add the unwritten-start-plus-unreadable-readback trace in the CHECKS child and require no start. An independent identity-write version should likewise require no clone.

   **Executed evidence:** `unconfirmed_start_note_must_not_start` failed against unchanged production modules: `start=Ok(())`, stored facts `[CloneIssued]`, recovery `Ok` with confirmed cleanup and no remaining recovery obligation.

2. **P1 / must-fix — Renewal can revive a lease that expired during the same synchronous method.** `src/disposable/tart/machine/steps.rs:462`, `:467`, `:472`, `:474`; `src/disposable/tart/machine/io.rs:141`, `:250`.

   **Failure scenario:** Reach Running at fake time zero and arm through second 3. At second 2.5 request renewal through second 5.5. Entry and `admit` both see the old lease live. A finite try-lock fake advances the shared clock by one second, returning at 3.5, safely inside its supplied 5.5 deadline. `guest_lease` uses `issue(..., destructive=false)`, so dispatch does not check the old lease again. The second lease is sent, `lease_until` is overwritten with 5.5, and the phase remains Running. The owner has revived an expired lease without ever entering Suspended.

   Checking stop/delete at their dispatch boundary fixes the reported destructive path, but it does not make the expired-lease hold irreversible across renewal's own inner calls.

   **Minimal fix:** Recheck the previous lease immediately before sending renewal and before accepting its result into `lease_until`. If it has expired, enter the existing hold and preserve that state. Add a time-advancing try-lock renewal case that requires Suspended, no replacement of the expired lease, and no subsequent task/destructive work.

   **Executed evidence:** `lease_must_not_renew_after_inner_expiry` failed: at second 3.5, renewal returned `Ok(())`, phase was Running, and the guest trace contained sequence 2 with deadline second 5.5 after sequence 1 ended at second 3.

3. **P2 / must-fix — The preparation aggregate starts after reserve, so the common throat still permits preparation past its actual end.** `src/disposable/tart/machine/io.rs:119`; `src/disposable/tart/machine/steps.rs:60`, `:124`, `:150`.

   **Failure scenario:** Shrink Prep to one second and enter reserve at zero with the normal five-second query deadline. A finite try-lock advances the clock to 0.5 seconds and returns before its supplied one-second deadline. Since `origin` is still None, each pre-reserve IO computes its prep end from its own current time: ledger rows, destination list, and reserve receive 1.5 seconds. Successful reserve then stores 0.5 as the origin. Advance to 1.1 seconds and clone: it succeeds with deadline 1.5, although the reserve-through-ready aggregate ended at second 1. All IO went through `issue`; the aggregate's missing anchor defeats its minimum.

   **Minimal fix:** Capture the reserve entry instant once, carry that prep origin through pre-reserve work, and retain it on success or ambiguous reservation. Do not replace it with a later `clock.now()`. Assert deadlines during reserve as well as rejecting a subsequent prep step after the original end. The existing clone clipping test begins timing after reserve and cannot catch this.

   **Executed evidence:** `prep_end_must_include_reserve` failed: ledger and runner deadlines were 1.5 seconds, and clone returned `Ok(())` at 1.1 seconds with a one-second preparation budget.

4. **P2 / should-fix — Recovery still quarantines a seal that committed, while the next reservation succeeds.** `src/disposable/tart/machine/recover.rs:106`, `:132`, `:153`; compare `src/disposable/tart/machine/cleanup.rs:219`.

   **Failure scenario:** Recover a never-started reserved row with authoritative absence, then inject `CommittedLost` on recovery's seal. The row becomes sealed. Recovery immediately attempts a quarantine append, which the sealed row refuses, then reads back the sealed state but publishes `Quarantined(ReceiptFailed)`, followups forbidden, and `recovery_required=false`. A new machine can reserve another id because the row is sealed without a quarantine fact. Ordinary attempt sealing now reconciles this case correctly; recovery does not share that behavior.

   **Minimal fix:** Apply the same verified-committed-seal outcome to recovery before attempting to quarantine it, or persist another consistent gate decision. Add recovery's committed-seal case. This remains should-fix under the requested bar: cleanup really was confirmed, no score survives, and no foreign or lease-expired destruction occurs.

   **Executed evidence:** `recovery_committed_seal_must_match_followup_gate` failed with exactly that quarantined receipt, a sealed row containing only `RecoveryNoted`, and the next reserve returning `Ok(())`.
