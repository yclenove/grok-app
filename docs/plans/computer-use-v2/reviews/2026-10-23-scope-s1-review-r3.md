**Residuals and assessed non-blockers — category (b), or explicitly deferred ownership**

- **Quiet-stream Stop wakeup remains incomplete on MCP adapters; this is subscription retirement, not an established Stop-release bypass.** `ScopedEvents` registers before checking liveness (`gcu-core/src/scope/events.rs:71`) and checks Stop before forwarding (`:50`, `:82`). Lease invalidation registers/wakes through `gcu-core/src/scope/model.rs:524`, `:584`; I found no lost lease wakeup in the registration-before-check sequence. Local `Activity` and `FakeUserActivity` implement Stop wakeups. `RemoteHost` still inherits the new no-op default (`gcu-host-api/src/activity.rs:69`; `gcu-mcp/src/host/remote.rs:203`), and `StopMirror::latch` records no wakers (`gcu-mcp/src/host/stop.rs:61`). A standalone quiet stream can consequently retain its inner subscription until another poll, potentially indefinitely. Nevertheless, its next poll refuses events while Stop is latched. The engine control handler independently latches and spawns input release without waiting for this stream or the session queue (`gcu-cli/src/engine.rs:370`, `:393`); native/Host waits and ordinary wait/settle loops also have control timers (`gcu-core/src/action/epoch.rs:52`; `gcu-core/src/wait/deadline.rs:17`). Therefore missing stream wakeup alone does not establish a Stop-priority or bounded-input-release failure on those paths. Acceptable as a residual under this review's calibration; not proof of bounded unsubscribe or real Stop timing. Narrow the ADR S1 row's unconditional “Stop wakes a Pending subscription” claim, or wire those adapters. The new quiet-stream regression uses only the waker-capable fake (`fixture_scope_round3.rs:65`).

- **Arming fixes the old pre-admission hole; the final OS delivery permit remains S2/S4.** `arm_attempt` subscribes before reading (`gcu-core/src/scope/authority.rs:66`); perform checks the expected epoch, admits, confirms, then selects invalidation before work (`gcu-core/src/scope/driver.rs:315`, `:325`, `:338`). Serializing control writers or publishing coherently would improve liveness.
