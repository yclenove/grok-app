1. **P1 — Recovery accepts VM-backed receipts and detaches without a stop barrier.**

   Evidence: [probe:3034](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3034), [probe:3223](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3223), [probe:3124](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3124), [probe:3381](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3381).

   `rec_parse` accepts C1/C2/C6/C7/C8/C9 receipts. Stage R skips Tart prechecks and never establishes VM/process absence before `rec_detach`, which can issue both normal and force. An unchanged receipt from a running C7 attempt therefore permits force while that VM runs. The receipt contains no clone incarnation or stop evidence from which recovery could prove otherwise. C5's no-VM property cannot be assumed for every accepted receipt.

   **Minimal fix:** limit this recovery slice to trusted C5 attempts and reject VM-backed receipts without effects. Supporting the other cases requires a persisted clone binding and a fresh stop/absence barrier before detach. Add an R test using an active C7 receipt; neither detach form nor unlink may occur.

2. **P1 — Recovery's force retry uses stale, incomplete mapping evidence.**

   Evidence: [probe:3098](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3098), [probe:3112](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3112), [probe:3126](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3126), [probe:3130](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3130).

   After a non-timeout normal failure, `rec_detach` immediately forces the same pathname. It does not refresh `hdiutil info`, recheck the image inode, or re-establish the stop barrier. The supplied file-holder measurement leaves approximately 10.7 seconds between the original check and force. Moreover, `rec_attached` only finds M among mount-point strings: it ignores every `dev-entry`, the saved device, and additional conflicting mounts. The ordinary cleanup helper performs a fresh `info_holds` before force; R bypasses that protection.

   **Minimal fix:** use one destructive-policy helper that verifies the complete I/device/M mapping, image and parent identity, and stop barrier immediately before each detach. For F3, derive the missing device from the fresh verified mapping. Quarantine on changed or extra mappings. Test a normal failure that changes the mapping/inode before force; force must be absent.

3. **P1 — “Force once” is enforced only within one invocation, not for the attempt.**

   Evidence: [probe:1714](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:1714), [probe:3026](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3026), [probe:3036](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3036), [probe:3130](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3130).

   The receipt records no force intent/count. `recovery=noted` is accepted on subsequent runs and does not restrict effects. If R's force fails, or recovery dies after sending it, running R on the same receipt authorizes another force. Receipts left by a failed C6 force have the same problem. This violates the attempt-wide limit and loses the evidence needed after a lost reply.

   **Minimal fix:** persist force intent before dispatch and accept at most one such intent per attempt across V/R invocations. A lost force reply must lead to read-only reconciliation or quarantine, without another force. Test repeated recovery after failed force and a crash immediately after force dispatch.

4. **P1 — Receipt containment does not establish a trusted original attempt or reject ancestor symlinks.**

   Evidence: [probe:3015](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3015), [probe:3040](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3040), [probe:3162](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3162), [probe:3247](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3247).

   Unknown/duplicate keys and lexical escapes are rejected, but any direct child is accepted as `attempt`, and any regular direct child as `image`; even the fixed `mailbox.sparseimage` basename is not required. A coherently edited receipt naming another file and its current dev/inode passes those checks and can unlink that file when it is absent from hdiutil's inventory. The only symlink checks cover final components. A symlink in the receipt directory or another ancestor is followed, and R never applies `out_ancestors_ok` to the recovery root or validates receipt ownership/mode/ACL. Thus lexical containment is not physical containment or original-attempt authorization.

   **Minimal fix:** validate the trusted receipt/root chain without following symlinks, including ownership/mode/ACL; enforce the fixed image name and registered attempt/mount binding. Check that binding against protected original registration, rather than accepting a self-consistent edited receipt as new authority. Add coherent image/sibling-attempt retargets and ancestor-symlink tests. This is wrong-receipt safety, not a claim of protection against a malicious same-UID owner.

5. **P1 — Recovery deletes the image before discovering a dirty or replaced M₀, and never audits A's remaining contents.**

   Evidence: [probe:3247](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3247), [probe:3255](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3255), [probe:3286](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3286).

   With I detached, recovery unlinks I before checking M₀'s identity or emptiness. A receipt whose mount directory was replaced, or whose M₀ contains a landed-write canary, therefore reaches quarantine only after losing the image. This also defeats the evidence-preserving behavior of C7 quarantine. The final A check only checks that I and M disappeared; an unexpected sibling can remain while R reports `ok`, contrary to the requested receipt/report-only remainder check.

   **Minimal fix:** preflight detached M₀ identity/emptiness and A's allowed contents before unlinking I. On unexpected content or identity, keep I and quarantine. Add R tests with a replaced M₀, a dirty M₀, and an unexpected A entry; assert the image survives.

6. **P1 — The shared removal worker does not satisfy the required parent-FD/no-follow deletion rule.**

   Evidence: [probe:2778](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2778), [probe:2782](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2782), [probe:2789](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2789), [probe:2792](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2792).

   **Inherited from the base, now used by the new recovery/case paths:** `volio_remove` stats parent and target pathnames, then calls `rm`/`rmdir` on the full pathname. It never pins a verified parent FD or traverses ancestors without following links. Parent replacement can redirect the final effect after both stat checks. The ADR explicitly says fresh checks do not bind the eventual effect; neither the receipt nor the added runbook instructions record the required exclusive host window.

   **Minimal fix:** make removal relative to a verified, pinned parent directory using no-follow traversal and recorded child identity, and require/record the ADR's exclusive window for the remaining child-name race. Add a synchronized parent-retarget test between check and removal; replacement contents must remain untouched. Reusing this helper is not sufficient evidence that rule 4 holds.

7. **P1 — C7 checks M₀ before the operations that can write into it.**

   Evidence: [probe:2515](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2515), [probe:2520](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2520), [probe:1920](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:1920), [tests:393](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/test_s11_0_probe.sh:393).

   After successful detach, `c7_list_m0` records emptiness before `run C7`. There is no subsequent M₀ observation while Tart remains alive. If guest writes land underneath the former mount during `c7ops`, the record still says `m0_entries=0`; cleanup unlinks the image first and only notices non-emptiness when rmdir fails. The supplied test plants its entry during detach, so it cannot catch this timing defect.

   **Minimal fix:** inspect M₀ identity/content after guest operations while the VM is still running, and recheck after stopping it before deleting evidence. A landed entry must produce `failed/m0-write` and preserve I/M₀. Move/add the test injection to the SSH C7 operation, after the initial empty observation.

8. **P1 — C7 marks failed or unmeasured guest/liveness checks as successful.**

   Evidence: [probe:2455](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2455), [probe:2473](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2473), [probe:2529](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2529), [probe:2541](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2541), [probe:1263](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:1263).

   Guest parse failure merely leaves `C7_GUEST=unknown`; `C7_ALIVE=no|absent|unknown` is also ignored. `C7_OK=1` is unconditional unless M₀ was already dirty. Successful volume cleanup then produces `ok/measured`, even after SSH exit 255 or a dead Tart VM. The parser also accepts contradictory counts and ignores extra output lines. Before detach, deployment/hash verification is followed directly by C7; there is no guest mailbox-slot readiness handshake, so post-detach errors need not have been caused by detach.

   **Minimal fix:** prove mailbox readiness before detach; require a complete, consistent guest observation, successful bounded execution, and `tart_alive=yes` before setting measurement-complete. Preserve measured ENOENT/EIO/hang classifications without treating missing evidence as success. Include VM cleanup/absence in the success condition. Add failed/timed-out/aborted SSH, malformed output, stopped Tart, and failed delete tests.

9. **P1 — C7's new `detached` cleanup path bypasses fresh attachment absence and VM proof before unlink.**

   Evidence: [probe:2513](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2513), [probe:2062](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2062), [probe:2090](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2090), [probe:1920](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:1920).

   C7 sets `VOL_PHASE=detached` while the VM runs. It subsequently performs SSH work and VM stop/delete/inventory. `volume_down` handles that phase by calling `unlink_image` directly; both `vm_proven_stopped` and `info_absent` are inside the separate `mounted` branch. The prior absence snapshot can therefore be tens of seconds old when unlink happens. A new attachment during that interval is not detected. The common `created` unlink path likewise has no absence check.

   **Minimal fix:** put a fresh authoritative attachment-absence check and the applicable VM barrier immediately before image unlink, for every phase/caller. Unknown or any attachment must quarantine. Add an attachment appearing during the C7 guest/teardown interval and assert no unlink.

10. **P1 — C6 and R bypass the shared cleanup deadline; receipt intake also has unbounded controller IO.**

    Evidence: [probe:588](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:588), [probe:2394](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2394), [probe:3016](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3016), [probe:3135](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3135), [probe:3455](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3455).

    C6 stops/deletes through `cleanup_owned`, but `VOL_SKIP_DOWN` clears `IN_CLEANUP` and `CLEANUP_DEADLINE` before holder startup, mapping checks, detach/force, reap, and unlink. Those operations use the 1500-second probe deadline and are not charged to the remaining cleanup allowance. R likewise runs all recovery effects outside cleanup accounting. Individual 5/20/10-second caps do not enforce the ADR's aggregate 60 seconds. Separately, `rec_parse` performs `wc -c` and shell reads directly on the supplied receipt before bounding its size; a huge or stalled filesystem file can block the controller outside any watchdog.

    **Minimal fix:** establish one absolute recovery/teardown deadline, retain it across C6's split cleanup, and include holder readiness/reap and every query/effect. Read/validate the receipt in a bounded worker with an 8193-byte capped read. Quarantine when the remaining budget cannot cover the next operation and reap. Test cumulative slow-but-individually-successful commands and stalled receipt IO; do not regress the measured need for a 20-second normal-detach allowance.

11. **P2 — C6 can report a busy-holder measurement after its holder has expired.**

    Evidence: [probe:2324](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2324), [probe:2409](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2409), [probe:2352](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2352), [probe:2361](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2361), [probe:2857](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2857).

    Readiness is checked once, then two `info_holds` sequences run before normal detach, with another before force. Each sequence contains multiple bounded plist/stat tools. The 30-second sleep can expire during these checks or detach, but neither detach path checks holder liveness again. `c6_reap_holder` treats an already naturally exited process as reaped and `C6_OK` can still become 1. The measurement no longer establishes that the file/dir was held during the operation.

    **Minimal fix:** track the holder's absolute expiry, validate liveness and sufficient remaining lifetime immediately before each measured detach, and classify premature expiry as incomplete. Keep the maximum 30 seconds. Add delayed mapping/detach tests and independently assert the holder PID is gone; the current tests only assert the script's own `holder_reaped=1` field.

12. **P2 — C10 invents `dest_count=0` for empty or incomplete successful output.**

    Evidence: [probe:2575](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2575), [probe:2589](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2589), [probe:2594](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2594).

    The counter starts at zero and is printed whenever `bad=0`, without requiring either the explicit no-destination sentence or a complete destination record. Exit 0 with no output, separator-only output, or just `Kind : Local` all become zero configured destinations. The brief requires parse failures to be `unknown`. The measured `tmutil: No destinations configured.` case itself is handled correctly.

    **Minimal fix:** require the explicit no-destination observation for zero, or validated destination records for a positive count; otherwise return unknown. Add empty/truncated/mixed-output fixtures alongside the measured no-destination shape.
