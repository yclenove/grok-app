1. **R2-1 — P1: holder expiry takes precedence over an indeterminate detach and loses the no-further-effects barrier.**

   Evidence: [probe:2481](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2481), [probe:2495](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2495), [probe:171](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:171), [probe:2565](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2565), [probe:1633](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:1633).

   After either detach, the new holder-dead branch runs **before** `st == 2` handling. It invokes `info_absent` and returns 2 as an ordinary holder-expired result, without setting `VOL_MAYBE` or quarantining. If detach's direct child could not be reaped, that query starts another `run_bounded`, which resets `REAP_UNKNOWN`. `m_run_c6` subsequently reaps the holder, clears cleanup mode, sets `HALT`, and permits outer cleanup. The changed `vm_proven_stopped` ignores `HALT`; a prior `cleanup.last` proving VM absence now suffices to allow another detach and image unlink while the earlier detach child remains unconfirmed.

   An extracted-function harness set detach to `st=2/REAP_UNKNOWN=1`, expired the holder, and returned a fresh still-attached inventory. The result was `holder-expired`, `VOL_MAYBE=0`, `REAP_UNKNOWN=0`; subsequent `volume_down` reached both another detach and unlink. All effects were inert doubles.

   **Minimal fix:** handle indeterminate detach/reap status before holder classification, quarantine without further effects, and retain a sticky unreaped-child prohibition that later successful queries or holder reaping cannot clear. Test holder exit coincident with normal and force timeout/unreap; no later detach, force, unlink, or rmdir may run.

2. **R2-2 — P1: force and V unlink still use checks from before additional blocking IO.**

   Evidence: [probe:3533](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3533), [probe:3342](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3342), [probe:3358](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3358), [probe:2150](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2150), [probe:2488](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2488), [probe:1985](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:1985).

   All three force sites verify mapping **before** `arm_force_intent`. That helper can spend up to 30 seconds capturing the receipt and another five appending intent. There is no mapping/VM recheck after it returns. V's `unlink_image` similarly checks absence first, then performs two separate five-second M₀/A workers before unlink. R orders its unlink preflight correctly, with `unlink_barrier` last. Thus changes during the added IO are not tested at dispatch, contrary to the controller's fresh-barrier ordering requirement.

   In-memory doubles changing mapping during intent persistence still reached force; changing attachment state during A preflight still reached V file removal. These demonstrate the missing final check, not a claim that rechecking eliminates the accepted pathname race.

   **Minimal fix:** persist force intent before the final mapping/VM check, and place V's final absence/VM check after M₀/A preflight, immediately before removal. A failed final check leaves intent spent and quarantines. Add mutations/stalls during intent persistence and preflight, asserting zero subsequent force/unlink.

3. **R2-3 — P1: newline filenames bypass the new attempt-content preflight, allowing evidence deletion before quarantine.**

   Evidence: [probe:3156](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3156), [probe:1957](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:1957), [probe:1995](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:1995), [probe:3694](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:3694).

   `volio_names` emits raw basenames separated by newlines. `a_allowed` skips empty lines and accepts repeated allowed names. An unexpected A entry named a newline therefore disappears from the audit; `mailbox.sparseimage` followed by a newline is another bypass. Neither requires changing the receipt or racing the owner window. V unlinks I and only discovers the stray entry when removing A fails. R can unlink I, remove M, keep A with that unexpected entry, and report recovered. The promised preflight preservation of I is lost in both cases.

   Feeding the actual `a_allowed` parser the serialized names for I, M, and an extra newline-named entry returned success.

   **Minimal fix:** reject line-breaking basenames in the bounded names worker before emitting any successful result, or use unambiguous NUL-delimited records. Add V/R detached fixtures containing newline-only and allowed-name-plus-newline entries; quarantine must preserve I.

4. **R2-4 — P1: C6 fallback teardown discards the host phase's absolute deadline.**

   Evidence: [probe:2415](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2415), [probe:2422](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2422), [probe:2565](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2565), [probe:2996](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2996), [probe:553](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:553).

   On holder expiry, `m_run_c6` calls `c6_host_end`, setting `CLEANUP_DEADLINE=0`, before outer `cleanup_owned` finishes the remaining volume work. That function creates a new deadline from `SECONDS + CLEANUP_LEFT`. `CLEANUP_LEFT` charges bounded command durations, not holder-readiness polling and all controller work, so this is not the original absolute limit. An extracted-function fake-clock check began with deadline 60 and reached expiry at time 55 with 10 charged seconds remaining; fallback received deadline **65**. The new budget fixture only refuses a detach whose cap cannot fit; it does not exercise cumulative work plus this fallback.

   **Minimal fix:** retain the same C6 host deadline through fallback cleanup and final holder reap, and clear it only when that phase is finished or permanently quarantined. Do not recreate it from charged time. Add cumulative slow-success/holder-expiry tests that assert the original absolute deadline on every later query and effect.

5. **R2-5 — P1: the new C7 consistency check rejects the real probe's hang result and accepts zero work.**

   Evidence: [probe:2706](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2706), [probe:2775](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2775), [run.rs:445](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/tools/s11-mailbox-probe/src/run.rs:445), [run.rs:451](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/tools/s11-mailbox-probe/src/run.rs:451), [bounded.rs:33](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/tools/s11-mailbox-probe/tests/bounded.rs:33).

   Rust stops after the first timed-out operation but always prints `ops=8`. A first-operation hang therefore produces `ops=8 ok=0 enoent=0 eio=0 other=0 hang=1`, with successful process exit. The new Bash parser requires the counters to sum to `ops`, so it discards that measured hang as `unknown/guest-malformed`. The added FIFO subprocess test explicitly asserts this incompatible producer output. Conversely, `ops=0` with all-zero counters passes the parser and can reach `ok/measured` without any operation.

   In-memory parsing accepted the ordinary eight-operation record, rejected the actual first-hang shape, and accepted the zero-operation shape.

   **Minimal fix:** align producer and consumer on the number of operations actually attempted, preserving stop-after-first-hang behavior; reject zero observations and incomplete records without a terminating hang. Feed the real Rust normal/ENOENT/EIO/hang records into the host parser in integration coverage, rather than testing each side against incompatible fixtures.

6. **R2-6 — P2: C7 can still report `ok/measured` when VM deletion failed.**

   Evidence: [probe:578](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:578), [probe:583](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:583), [probe:2799](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2799), [probe:1455](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:1455).

   `finish_c7` checks only the third cleanup field, `absent=yes`. If delete removes the clone but returns nonzero, cleanup records `ok fail yes`; volume cleanup can complete and the case still becomes `ok/measured`. The stage fold can likewise be `ok`. The global `ANY_CLEANUP_FAILED` flag makes the top-level exit fail, so this is an inconsistent successful case/stage classification rather than a silent overall pass. The extracted finalizer reproduced that result.

   **Minimal fix:** require successful stop, successful delete, and confirmed absence in C7's final success condition. Test a delete effect followed by a nonzero exit, and require a non-successful case/stage verdict with a cleanup reason.

7. **R2-7 — P2: C6 does not revalidate holder lifetime before force.**

   Evidence: [probe:2471](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2471), [probe:2488](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2488), [probe:2495](/home/claude/src/grok-app-wt/s11-m2cb/computer-use/scripts/s11-0-probe.sh:2495).

   The lifetime check covers only normal detach. After its busy failure, fresh inventory/stat/plist work and receipt capture/append can consume the rest of the 30-second hold. Force is then dispatched without checking that the holder still exists or has enough lifetime for force's own cap. The later liveness check correctly downgrades the measurement, but it cannot make that force a busy-holder observation. A helper harness expiring the holder during the second mapping check still dispatched force with `holder_live=0`.

   **Minimal fix:** immediately before force, check holder liveness and remaining lifetime against force's cap, alongside the final mapping/VM checks. If insufficient, finish as incomplete/holder-expired and use the same remaining teardown deadline for permitted cleanup. Add delayed mapping and delayed intent-write cases; retain the 30-second holder maximum and independently verify the PID is gone.
